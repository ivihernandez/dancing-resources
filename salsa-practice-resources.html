Phase 1: Step Forward & Backward Practicing

The basic: lead steps forward on 1, back on 5. Stay on your line (the slot).

Your line vs. her line: In On1 (LA style), you dance on a straight line called the slot. The lead and follow each have their own track — like two rails of a train track. You step forward/back on your rail; she steps forward/back on hers. You never cross into each other's track during the basic.

Phase 3: Cross-Body Lead (Change of Position) Practicing

You and she swap places. On count 5 you step your right foot back to open the door; on counts 5-6-7 she walks across. Your lines trade.

The slot swap: Think of it as two train tracks. During the basic, you each stay on your track. During the cross-body lead, you step off your track (step back and to the side on 5) to let her walk across from her track to yours. Then you step onto where her track was. You've swapped sides of the slot. The "railway tracks" analogy from Dance Dojo explains this best.
